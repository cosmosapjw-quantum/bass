# SciSpace literature lock — SYNC-MAP-02A

This literature is admitted only for semantic/provenance methodology.

- OpenMath content dictionaries: stable identifiers and content-level mathematical meaning rather than display strings.
- W3C MathML: separation of presentation markup and content semantics.
- W3C PROV-DM: explicit entities, generation activities, ownership, sources and derivation relations.
- Soiland-Reyes et al., “Packaging research artefacts with RO-Crate,” *Data Science* 5 (2022) 97–138, DOI `10.3233/DS-210053`: machine-readable identifiers, provenance, relations and replayable research objects.
- Agullo, Olmedo and Sreenath, “xAct Implementation of the Theory of Cosmological Perturbation in Bianchi I Spacetimes,” *Mathematics* 8 (2020) 290, DOI `10.3390/math8020290`: reproducible Wolfram/xAct organization of anisotropic-cosmology calculations.

The project semantic hash excludes formula identity, theorem label and provenance, but retains assumptions, domain, dimensions, mathematical terms, structural-zero rules, branch predicates and limits. Hash equality is therefore a candidate equivalence signal only. It cannot override convention, representation or adapter differences and does not by itself establish cross-repository equality.
