# SciSpace literature lock — GEO-02 connection methodology

**Search question:** Which peer-reviewed studies support deriving the
Levi–Civita connection in a non-coordinate orthonormal frame from
Lie-algebra/commutator data, and its use in spatially homogeneous Bianchi
cosmology?

## Admitted methodological references

1. H. van Elst and C. Uggla, “General Relativistic 1+3 Orthonormal Frame
   Approach Revisited,” arXiv:`gr-qc/9603026`.

   Admitted use: the orthonormal-frame decomposition and specialization of
   general relativistic equations to spatially homogeneous cosmology. It does
   not fix the BASS index order or signs.

2. M. Lachieze-Rey, “Connections and Frame Bundle Reductions,”
   arXiv:`2002.01410`.

   Admitted use: the characterization of the Levi–Civita connection as the
   unique torsion-free connection preserving the orthogonal metric structure.
   It is not a source for Bianchi branch normalizations.

3. A. Yu. Kamenshchik, “The Bianchi Classification of the
   Three-Dimensional Lie Algebras and Homogeneous Cosmologies and the
   Mixmaster Universe,” DOI `10.1007/978-3-030-18061-4_3`.

   Admitted use: methodological support for treating the Bianchi labels as
   three-dimensional Lie-algebra classes and for retaining I, II and IX as
   canonical witnesses.

4. Z. Avetisyan and R. Verch, “Explicit harmonic and spectral analysis in
   Bianchi I–VII type cosmologies,” *Classical and Quantum Gravity* **30**
   155006, DOI `10.1088/0264-9381/30/15/155006`.

   Admitted use: support for a uniform treatment of left-invariant geometric
   structures and automorphism actions across Bianchi groups. Harmonic and
   perturbative results are outside GEO-02.
