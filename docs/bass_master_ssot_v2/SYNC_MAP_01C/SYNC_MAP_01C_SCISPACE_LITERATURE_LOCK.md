# SciSpace literature lock — SYNC-MAP-01C

The following references are admitted only as methodological support. They do
not own BASS conventions, signs, branch equations or scientific claims.

1. I. Agullo, J. Olmedo and V. Sreenath, “xAct Implementation of the Theory of
   Cosmological Perturbation in Bianchi I Spacetimes,” *Mathematics* 8 (2020)
   290, DOI `10.3390/math8020290`.

   Admitted use: reproducible Wolfram/xAct organization of anisotropic
   cosmology tensor calculations and machine checks.

2. C. Pitrou, X. Roy and O. Umeh, “xPand: An algorithm for perturbing
   homogeneous cosmologies,” arXiv:`1302.6174`.

   Admitted use: separation of abstract 3+1 identities, homogeneous-background
   specialization and generated component equations. Perturbative formulas are
   outside this stage.

3. S. Soiland-Reyes et al., “Packaging research artefacts with RO-Crate,”
   *Data Science* 5 (2022) 97–138, DOI `10.3233/DS-210053`.

   Admitted use: machine-readable research packages should carry identifiers,
   provenance, relations and annotations. This supports the exact source
   manifest, replay entrypoint and linked Git/Dropbox/Atlassian receipts.

4. E. Ó Carragáin, C. Goble, P. Sefton and S. Soiland-Reyes, “A lightweight
   approach to research object data packaging,” DOI
   `10.5281/zenodo.3250687`.

   Admitted use: source, inputs, provenance and relations should be retained as
   a reconstructable research object.

None of these references authorizes the BASS Riemann-sign adapter, the
connection index ordering, the I/V/IX curvature values, all-type support,
Einstein–matter evolution, REC/REI compatibility, or numerical/scientific
promotion. Those remain project-owned exact derivations and tests.
