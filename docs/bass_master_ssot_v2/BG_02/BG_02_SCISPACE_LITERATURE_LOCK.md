# BG-02 SciSpace literature lock

## Admitted methodological sources

1. H. van Elst and C. Uggla, *General relativistic orthonormal frame
   approach*, Classical and Quantum Gravity 14 (1997),
   DOI `10.1088/0264-9381/14/9/021`, arXiv `gr-qc/9603026`.

   Admitted for the organization of general-relativistic orthonormal-frame
   variables and spatially homogeneous specialization.  It does not override
   BASS signs, dimensions or index order.

2. R. T. Jantzen, *Spatially Homogeneous Dynamics: A Unified Picture*,
   arXiv `gr-qc/0102035`.

   Admitted for the structural organization of spatially homogeneous
   dynamics.  It does not supply project-specific formula identity.

3. S. Frittelli, *Note on the propagation of the constraints in standard
   (3+1) general relativity*, Physical Review D 55, 5992 (1997),
   DOI `10.1103/PhysRevD.55.5992`.

   Admitted for the methodological point that preservation of Hamiltonian and
   momentum constraints is a separate Bianchi-identity/evolution problem.
   Deriving the constraints is not equivalent to proving their numerical
   propagation.

4. G. Yoneda and H. Shinkai, *Constraint propagation in the family of ADM
   systems*, Physical Review D 63, 124019 (2001),
   DOI `10.1103/PhysRevD.63.124019`, arXiv `gr-qc/0103032`.

   Admitted for the distinction between an on-shell-equivalent Einstein
   formulation and the off-shell propagation/amplification properties of its
   constraint residuals.  Adding constraints to evolution equations can
   change numerical behavior without changing the exact Einstein solution
   set.

index convention, nor the connection storage adapter.  Those are owned by the
exact W2/W3 BASS source line and executable receipts.
