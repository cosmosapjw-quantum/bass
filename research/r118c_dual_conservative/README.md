## C1-C4: mathematical contract
In each moving emission-coordinate log-frequency cell u=ln(h nu_emit/k_B T_emit), x=exp(u), represent the positive screen number measure e^(3u)F(u)du by two endpoint masses A=(x_R N-E)/(x_R-x_L), B=(E-x_L N)/(x_R-x_L), where N=int e^(3u)F du, E=int e^(4u)F du. Positivity follows from x_L N <= E <= x_R N in Loewner order. Integrate these hats by positive 8-point Gauss-Legendre quadrature; shared endpoint masses are summed. This is a discrete measure, not a smooth occupation reconstruction. Preserve finite initial-domain truncation.

Normal angular-integrated centered number Q_i = w_i exp(3(s_i+eta))/(4 pi N3) times the emission-coordinate nodal tensor, N3=2 zeta(3), N4=pi^4/15. Centered material frequency x_m=exp(u+s+eta) D, D=gamma(1-beta.e). Material source contribution D_j J_j Q_j J_j^T follows from D^3 number boost and D^-2 solid angle. Gain to ray i is area_i GAIN Jinv_i P_i A^-1/2 [source] (.)^T, where area_i=w_i/D_i^2; loss D_i Q_i. The gain is normalized in the material frame with the existing positive quadrature map. Deposit each donor onto bracketing destination nodes in LINEAR material energy, with w_R=expm1(z-z_L)/expm1(z_R-z_L). Sum weights=1 and sum weighted energies=incoming energy. Outside the convex hull, record all of the gain as escaped number/energy; no reinjection. Independent cold material-frame energy audit is done before deposition, with raw gain and loss; the actual projected change and escapes determine projection defect. Normal energy uses the same material-energy exchange divided by destination D. BE/SDIRK2 solve a single projected nodal generator with bounded fixed-point stages. Cap per-cell depth*max D at 1. No implicit stage clipping. Free transport updates shift, angular area, screen only. Observer boost shifts nodes, transforms solid angles and screens, not their emission-coordinate masses.

Cumulative local numerical squared-energy moment increase: for an incoming x and a<=x<=b, (w_L a^2+w_R b^2-x^2)=(x-a)(b-x)>=0. Count the contribution of each scattering gain, weighted by the actual SDIRK stage. This measures remap diffusion on the normal hypersurface with local centered material energy squared, not a global conserved charge or heating. Wolfram verified both weights and the quadratic difference.

Line true emission/absorption uses actual node energy and positive exponential relaxation at the node, with constant source times nodal phase-space weight. Explicit velocity knots split the time mesh. This eliminates projection energy defect but node sampling does not resolve an arbitrary narrow profile. Independent continuum and nodal characteristic oracles distinguish those errors.

Dilute frequency diffusion: for q=photon number per material log frequency, equilibrium pi(zeta) proportional to exp(3 zeta-h nu/kT_b), one possible reversible generator has flux -K*pi*d(q/pi)/dzeta. Rybicki 2006 (arXiv:astro-ph/0603047, Eq. 20) gives the frequency-space Fokker-Planck model including the recoil, phase-space and stimulated terms. In the dilute limit its transformation to log frequency yields the 3 phase-space factor; if J is number per unit nu, q=nu J and K=(c chi Delta nu_D^2/2)*D_profile(nu)/nu^2 in material time units. The implemented K is instead a prescribed Gaussian log-frequency envelope (peak diffusion in material ray clock per H). The pair rate sum is 2 D K / du^2. Nodal equilibrium pi_j=hat_volume_j exp(-h nu_j/kT_b); rates ratio pi_R/pi_L, positive exact pair exponential. Even/odd/even Strang sweep, zero external frequency flux. This is a consistent smooth-limit reversible model, not a calibrated physical coefficient, Voigt profile, angular scattering, stimulated Bose term or atom recoil kernel. Positive pair maps keep the common polarized Wien measure stationary. Energy exchanged with prescribed bath is measured from signed transfers times node-energy differences, separate from line absorption and remap error. A stiff pair step can be positive without being uniformly accurate for the full chain.
