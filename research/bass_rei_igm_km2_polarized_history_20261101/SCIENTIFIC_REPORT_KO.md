## Derived physics: two distinct optical legs + one gain event

Original emitted photon packet `(i,a)` at `s_i`, incident ray direction `a`, is first scattered at edge `u_j` and observed in outgoing direction `β` at `U`. One exact original Thomson P8 screen contribution is proportional to

`B_{j i a β}^{AB}(U) = ω_j^(Simpson) [3 ν_j/(8π)] × A_i(a) × e^{-4(R_j(a)-R_i(a))} × e^{-[τ_j(a)-τ_i(a)]-[τ_U(β)-τ_j(β)]} × [source angular/boost and screen projector]^{AB}`.

Here `ν_j=n_H x_e σ_T c T_star`, all original source arrays and positive angular weights are retained. For a bounded perturbation `n_e(u)↦n_e(u)[1+ε f(u)]` at fixed geometry, emission and electron velocity, **derive exactly** (derivative at ε=0):

`δ B_{ji aβ}/B_{ji aβ} = f(u_j) − ∫_{s_i}^{u_j}h(u,a) f(u)du − ∫_{u_j}^{U}h(u,β) f(u)du`.

This is a contributionwise identity: the P8 angular sum and 2×2 screen must still be performed to obtain an observable response. Because `a` and `β` differ after scattering, replacing the two optical legs by an unscattered same-ray optical depth is **not** valid. The full original P8 3×3 electron-rest moment and transported 2×2 outgoing screen were implemented without cloning/re-solving any Einstein model.

Use a *declared discrete perturbation model*: 40 piecewise-constant `δ ln n_e` modes on original time panels. At the exact right-end scattering source node `u_j`, the perturbed scattering rate takes the left limit from panel `j−1`. This convention makes the discrete derivative uniquely defined but is not a claim about continuous cosmic x_e discontinuities; a future REI source import will require interpolation/clock matching. The original per-ray Gauss optical increments are kept without rescaling atomic photoionization/Peebles emission.

For each original source event `j`, the incident electron-frame source-moment response through panel `m` is `-δM_{j,m} = -½ Σ_{i,a} w_{j,a}D_{j,a}² p_{i→j,a} K_pre(m;i,j,a) (F_{j,a}F_{j,a}ᵀ)`, and the outgoing screen response obeys

`δB_{j,m}^{AB}(β) = B_j^{AB}(β) [1_(m=j−1) − K_post(m;j,U,β)] − C_{j,β} [F_{j,β}ᵀ δM_{j,m} F_{j,β}]^{AB}`.

The source lifetime weighting `s_i` yields an additional exact emission-time moment; weighting the collision event by `u_j` yields the mean first-scatter time. Gauge-invariant linear polarization intensity `L_β = sqrt((B11−B22)² + 4B12²)` is summed with the original sky quadrature and divided by summed intensity. Ray-specific Stokes Q/U derivatives are published separately, and no invalid cross-ray addition of local-basis Q/U is performed.
