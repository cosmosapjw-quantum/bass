## C1-C4. Equations and implementation choice
Reference temperature T_ref=T_emit exp(-eta); xi=ln(T/T_ref). B_p=int exp(p xi)dP,
M_k=int xi^k dP. The dimensionless carriers B3/B4 are physical photon number
and bolometric energy divided by their Planck emission normalizations and
multiplied by exp(3 eta)/exp(4 eta). All five are transverse symmetric tensors.
Transport: A0 Bp+p Rtilde Bp, A0 Mk+k Rtilde M_(k-1), Rtilde=-Sigma:ee.
This uses exact carrier derivative at p=0 of the centered Lie generator.
No finite differencing in p. Initial B3=B4=M0=P/2, M1=M2=0.
Material D=gamma(1-beta.e), J screen isometry; boost Bp by D^p J(.)J^T,
Mk by binomial log-D shift plus screen isometry. Inverse shift on increments.
Each of five material carriers solves the same implicit 6x6 gain matrix
with independent RHS. SDIRK combines increments. Gain normalization is
recomputed on this spectral angular mesh, NOT copied from original B4 event.
Elastic scattering is linear and energy-independent; source for B4 alone is
not the source for number or log moments. No spectral collision approximation
based on y-distortion. Harmonic transport is native; spectral collision uses
angular grid even if history was generated by R116-A continuum route.
Fixed accepted recoil velocities: independently evolved B4 compared with
original B4, not a second self-consistent recoil solve.

Collision normal-frame dBp/dtau=D^(1-p) J^-1 C_rest J^-T.
dOmega_u=D^-2 dOmega_n. Trace conservation in material frame implies
int dOmega_n delta B3=0 and int dOmega_n D delta B4=0, equivalent to
normal delta E=beta.delta q at frozen beta. These are checked per event.
For centered number N and number flux F, N'=2 A.F since angular divergence
is 3 Sigma:ee+2 A.e. Class A N constant; class B continuity checked with
endpoint trapezoid (not exact discrete integral).
Scalar m0=tr M0, mean=tr M1/m0, v=tr M2/m0-mean^2. y_M=(ln(tr B4/m0)
-4/3 ln(tr B3/m0))/4 >=0 for positive measures; v/2 only leading order.
Observer boost shifts mean by log D but not v or y_M. Positive log Gram
[[M0,M1],[M1,M2]] on 2D screen is necessary but not sufficient for existence
of the measure; finite harmonic truncation and SDIRK can violate positivity.
CLI flags necessary checks on sampled accepted normal histories at absolute
dimensionless tolerance 1e-9. Warning retained, no clipping, no universal
angular positivity certificate, no inference admission on warnings.

arXiv:1402.0968, mixing of blackbodies A&A 543 A77 (2012).
