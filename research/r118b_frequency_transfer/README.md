## Mathematical contract

Use occupation coherency in a transverse polarization screen. Each ray has an emission log-frequency coordinate u = ln(h nu_emit / k_B T_emit) and a geometric shift s from the accepted ray propagation. In the normal frame log(h nu/k_B T_emit) = u+s; in the instantaneous material frame add ln D, D = gamma(1-beta.e). Angular weights transform as D^-2 and the screen by congruence. Free transport changes shifts, directions, areas and screens without frequency projection. At a frozen collision, material occupation is compared at the same frequency on the union of all shifted bin boundaries. Destination weights integrate exp(3z) exactly over overlaps; zero exterior is explicitly accounted as boundary escape.

The old reference method projects a resolved union-partition collision resolvent onto the ray bins at the end (union). The stage-consistent method instead evolves the projected generator P L P (projected), using the same finite-volume gain operator at every implicit BE or SDIRK2 stage. These are different discretizations. With P^2=P and smooth bounded L, P R(hL) P - R(hPLP) on range(P) has leading h^2 PL(I-P)LP/2 for an order-two integrator. Projected stages solve Y = T input + alpha G_FV(Y) by bounded linear fixed-point iteration with explicit residual. SDIRK or projected BE cells are subdivided to cap max D*depth at 1, with bounded subcell count. Failure to converge is an error, not a clipped answer. The CLI defaults to projected only when the new frequency path is enabled; library replay_frequency remains the union-reference convenience function.

For a prescribed Gaussian phi normalized in dlog frequency, constant isotropic occupation source S, and strength kappa, the normal-clock line rate is D kappa phi. A finite volume uses I_p = integral exp(p u) phi du; W_3 = integral exp(3u)du. Its exponential transmission uses tau = delta eta D kappa I_3/W_3. The integrated raw energy source uses W_3 (1-exp(-tau)) I_4/I_3, not an inferred heating from the reconstruction error. Profile-aware positive quadrature, expm1, and explicit velocity-knot splitting handle narrow and thick lines. No Sobolev inverse derivative, sign clipping, or resonant redistribution. The prescribed line is true absorption/emission in a frozen medium; its source is not derived from a level population. A quadratic tangency z-z_L=a(t-t*)^2 has tau/(D kappa) = Gamma(1/4)/(2^(5/4) sqrt(pi |a| sigma)) in the infinite-window frozen-coefficient model. Finite sigma regularizes the turning point; the narrow-line limit is nonuniform. Tested independently via SciPy quadrature and Wolfram symbolic integration.

Number and energy use Planck normalizations 2 zeta(3), pi^4/15, with angular average and centered redshift factors exp(3 eta), exp(4 eta). Material-energy conservation refers to the normal hypersurface integral weighted by D, before remapping. Number-weighted constant reconstruction does not preserve the energy integral exactly. All numerical projection changes are separately recorded, never assigned to atomic heating. Geometric work is pre/post bookkeeping, not independent verification of Einstein energy exchange. Class-B number continuity is a trapezoidal flux diagnostic. All positivity observations are sampled, not a full proof for arbitrary inputs.
