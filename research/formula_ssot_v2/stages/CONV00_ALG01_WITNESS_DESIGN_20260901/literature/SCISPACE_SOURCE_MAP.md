# SciSpace literature regression map

## Project authority

The existing project photon SSOT and literal eleven-branch atlas remain authority for signs, normalization, Bianchi decomposition, branch predicates, exceptional-sector conventions and strict exclusions. This stage imports them rather than replacing them.

## External regressions

1. C. Pitrou, X. Roy and O. Umeh, “xPand: An algorithm for perturbing homogeneous cosmologies,” *Classical and Quantum Gravity* 30 (2013) 165002, DOI `10.1088/0264-9381/30/16/165002`, arXiv:`1302.6174`.
   - Supports a coordinate-independent geometrical/xAct workflow for homogeneous cosmologies.
   - Does not own this project’s signs, normalization or branch registry.

2. C. G. Hewitt, J. T. Horwood and J. Wainwright, “Asymptotic dynamics of the exceptional Bianchi cosmologies,” *Classical and Quantum Gravity* 20 (2003) 1743, DOI `10.1088/0264-9381/20/9/311`, arXiv:`gr-qc/0211071`.
   - Regression source for exceptional `VI_-1/9` and its extra dynamical freedom.
   - Does not replace the project’s displayed exceptional constraints or chart conventions.
