# Equation-to-code and numerical design

New isolated module: bianchi/nonperturbative/einstein_tabulated_source.py. TabulatedRadiation validates exact-binary edge/energy inputs and constructs uniform solid-angle cells with eight implicit coordinate reflections. Integer refinement divides original cell intervals and energies exactly using Fraction. This preserves the physical piecewise-constant source. Cell means/ranges are MPFR192 enclosures; actual numerical ray components and masses are retained as binary64 without renormalization.

moments computes v sum mass*r and v b_i sum mass*n_i^2/r; rhs computes ell'=h,h'=-theta*h+P. initial_state uses the actual floating source E(0) and declared tracefree shear. certified_tabulated_pressure implements THEORY.md's Taylor variance, stored-ray and stored-mass terms. certified_tabulated_energy separates exact continuum sum, exact-real stored-ray sum and its floating evaluation. certified_tabulated_geometry constructs the common expanding box and propagates the pressure error with the inherited MPFR Dini solver. Output explicitly excludes the time-integrator defect.

Required edge cases: backend must be explicit; finite real ordered partitions must exactly span [0,1]; shape must match cells; energy>=0 and positive finite total; refinement integer in bounded supported range; zero-energy cells skipped; no nonfinite or unresolved cell enclosures; no vanishing stored mass/ray norm; valid nonnegative geometry box; finite positive end time; exact-binary sx,sy reference with sz=-(sx+sy). MPFR trig range must include the sin(pi*t) maximum at t=1/2, with exact special endpoints; cos(pi*t) uses its correct monotonic domain.

Independent references: separately integrate the original cells with tensor Gauss-Legendre quadrature; do not call cell-mean or source-certificate helpers. Compare same physical table at refinements1,2,4,8. Include nonuniform cells, strongly nonuniform energies, isotropic all-octant table, finite-amplitude boxes, diagonal permutation, finite differences of energy continuity, coupled nonlinear IVP and initial Hamiltonian residual. A midpoint-ray mutation must violate the zero-geometry barycenter contract. Sign/normalization mutations must be rejected by source/energy checks.
