# R3 | R118-C3Y BASS

At fixed native mesh h and signed control epsilon, B is a complex vector in the same m convention. For B0, B1 not automatically zero, E(t)=(B(+e)+B(-e))/2-B(0), O(t)=(B(+e)-B(-e))/2. Exact parity in sign of amplitude is even/odd splitting, not sky parity. Existing odd division e^3 presumes B1=0; test B1/e separately and do not infer it from fits.
