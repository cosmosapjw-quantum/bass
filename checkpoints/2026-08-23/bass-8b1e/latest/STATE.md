## Objective (frozen)

Implement and verify a bounded Python authority that lifts the instantaneous
E3 cold-Thomson validity decision onto a finite Q-Hubble-time trajectory.  It
must bind electron, hard photon-energy, electron-temperature, source-frame,
normal-Hubble, and local ray-rate support to one explicit common coordinate;
compute conservative per-segment energy/temperature suprema including
interior extrema of a linearly interpolated electron number current; and keep
future kinetic evolution distinct from past-light-cone optical-depth
accumulation.  Stop before the direction-dependent Q collision generator,
production solver/JAX/Rust wiring, finite-ell hierarchy, or authority-row
promotion.

## Frozen conventions and bounded design

1. Metric signature is `(-,+,+,+)` and photon directions are future
   propagation directions in the named tetrad.
2. `tau_Q` is dimensionless Q Hubble time and increases with physical time:
   `dt_phys/dtau_Q = 1/H_normal`, with `H_normal>0` in `s^-1`.
3. This node accepts only an explicit identity binding into one
   `q_hubble_time_tau` coordinate authority.  Redshift, conformal time, affine
   time, or nonidentity coordinate conversions are rejected rather than
   inferred; their adapters are separate authority work.
4. Canonical source arrays are finite, immutable, and strictly increasing in
   `tau_Q`; solver traversal may be forward or reverse without reversing the
   stored arrays.
5. Source-frame hard photon-energy maxima are in joules; electron temperature
   is in kelvin; `H_normal` is in `s^-1`; `nu_tau=d optical_depth/dtau_Q` is
   dimensionless per dimensionless `tau_Q`.
6. `x=E_e/(m_e c^2)` and `theta_e=k_B T_e/(m_e c^2)`.  Explicit CODATA/SI
   `c`, `k_B`, and `m_e c^2` remain owned by `bianchi.physical.units`.
7. Independent-electron schedules retain their existing piecewise-linear
   number-current semantics.  Comoving schedules retain linear proper density
   and use a separately provenance-bound live-fluid beta profile; the frame
   transform does not change either closure into the other.
8. On each union segment, the independent-electron normal gamma maximum is
   obtained from the rational quadratic generated by linear `N_e^A` and its
   interior stationary point.  The relative-frame gamma is conservatively
   bounded by

   `Gamma_rel <= gamma_e,max gamma_s,max (1+b_e,max b_s,max)`.

   This is deliberately conservative, not claimed exact when both frames
   move.
9. Only non-vacuum segments enter the cold-Thomson energy budget.  Vacuum
   segments remain collision-off, but all profile syntax, coordinate,
   provenance, and support checks still run and cannot erase adjacent
   non-vacuum requirements.
10. Orientation types are:

    ```text
    FUTURE_KINETIC_IVP:
        dT_local/dtau_Q = +nu_tau, traversed tau_start <= tau_end

    PAST_LIGHT_CONE_ACCUMULATION:
        kappa(tau)=integral_tau^tau_observer nu_tau dtau
        dkappa/dtau_Q = -nu_tau, traversed tau_start >= tau_end
    ```

    Both return the same non-negative depth for opposite traversal of one
    physical segment; unsupported role/direction combinations raise.
