# Transplant map

## Retained concepts

From the research harness: evidence/claim separation, phase gates, negative-result
preservation, survivor-only promotion, and bounded adversarial review.

From the coding harness: task contract, scientific invariants, validation ladder,
independent review, failure semantics, and code/research handoff separation.

From the low-ell materials: docs-are-not-evidence, family registry versus genuine runtime
support, global tilt/local boost separation, exact electron-frame collision honesty,
same-physics optimization, deterministic artifact packaging, and claim locks.

## Rewritten

- `low-ell` becomes finite configurable high-ell with joint convergence obligations.
- Perturbation hierarchy becomes homogeneous background angular transport.
- Descriptive DAG becomes a machine-readable prerequisite/invalidation graph.
- Permanent runtime lock becomes feature/family claim promotion from evidence while
  perturbation and likelihood remain permanently out of v1 scope.
- Plot loops become conditional on numerical/observable information value.
- Patch/audit loops are bounded to two repairs and re-enter affected gates.
- Runtime interruption uses durable receipts rather than transcript inheritance.
