# Web research notes

Primary-source checks used for the design boundary:

1. Portsmouth & Bertschinger, *A tensor formalism for transfer and Compton
   scattering of polarized light*, arXiv:astro-ph/0412094.  The polarization
   tensor is designed for Lorentz-frame boosts, and the collision equation is
   derived first in the electron rest frame in the Thomson limit.
2. Challinor, *Microwave background polarization in cosmological models*,
   arXiv:astro-ph/9911481.  Provides the exact 1+3 covariant, observer-dependent
   polarized-radiation hierarchy context.
3. Pitrou, *The radiative transfer at second order: a full treatment of the
   Boltzmann equation with polarization*, arXiv:0809.3036.  Uses a tensor-valued
   distribution and explicit collision term.
4. Dai & Chluba, *New operator approach to the CMB aberration kernels in
   harmonic space*, arXiv:1403.6117.  Treats Lorentz boosts on the full
   spin-weighted sky and supports the policy that a finite-l truncation is not
   silently promoted to an exact finite boost representation.

These papers support the frame/carrier architecture.  They do not by themselves
prove the project-specific discrete coefficients; those are checked by the
frozen BASS owners, exact witnesses, and numerical audits.
