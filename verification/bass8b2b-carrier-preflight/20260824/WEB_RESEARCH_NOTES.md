# External research notes

Primary literature used for the carrier decision:

1. L. Dai and J. Chluba, *New operator approach to the CMB aberration kernels
   in harmonic space*, arXiv:1403.6117. Exact Lorentz boosts act on the full
   spin-weighted sky-function Hilbert space and harmonic coefficients mix over
   multipoles. This supports rejecting a finite-l truncation as an exact boost
   representation.
2. J. Portsmouth and E. Bertschinger, *A tensor formalism for transfer and
   Compton scattering of polarized light*, arXiv:astro-ph/0412094. The
   polarization tensor is designed for covariant frame transformations and
   electron-rest scattering, supporting the basis-free screen-tensor carrier.
3. V. I. Lebedev and D. N. Laikov, *A quadrature formula for the sphere of the
   131st algebraic order of accuracy*, Doklady Mathematics 59 (1999) 477–481.
   Lebedev rules provide symmetric rest-frame spherical quadratures; project
   claims of finite-boost structure preservation come from the paired map and
   direct tests, not from quadrature exactness alone.
